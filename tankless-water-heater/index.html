Why scale is the main enemy in Cypress

Golden State Water reports an average hardness of 237 mg/L, or about 13.8 grains per gallon, for its West Orange County system. Readings in its 2025 data ranged from roughly 63 to 369 mg/L.

Tankless heaters run water through a narrow heat exchanger, so scale narrows the passage and the unit overheats or throttles flow. Descaling is a cleaning flush that circulates a mild acid through the exchanger to dissolve the buildup.

The flush is a maintenance visit, not a repair, and it is the reason callers ask whether their unit is dying when it is only dirty.

Tankless versus a tank: an honest comparison

Tankless units save floor space, heat water only when it is used and last longer than most tanks. They also cost more to buy and install, and a household that opens several showers at once may need a larger model.

For an older home with a cramped garage closet, the wall mounting is a real advantage. For a tight budget, a standard tank swap through water heater replacement is simpler.

Sizing a tankless unit for a Cypress household

Tankless sizing depends on two numbers: how many gallons per minute you need at the same moment, and how much the incoming water has to be heated. A shower and a kitchen faucet running together might call for four to five gallons per minute. Add a dishwasher or washing machine and the number climbs.

Cold-season incoming water is cooler, so the same unit delivers less hot water in winter than it does in summer. A technician adds up your fixtures and picks a unit that holds up on the coldest morning, not the warmest afternoon.

Maintenance habits that extend tankless life

  • Flush the heat exchanger on the schedule in your manual, and more often if the water leaves heavy spotting on glass.
  • Clean the inlet screen so debris does not restrict flow.
  • Keep the area around the vent clear of storage and debris.
  • Check the condensate drain on condensing models.
  • Note any error code and the time it appeared before you call.

Standards and checking a plumber's license

Plumbing work for tankless water heater in California is governed by the California Plumbing Code, and plumbing contractors must hold a license from the Contractors State License Board.
