Plumbing in a city shaped by Rossmoor and the base

Los Alamitos was incorporated on March 1, 1960, and covers about four square miles with roughly 11,800 residents. Rossmoor surrounds it on the north, east and west. Ross Cortese began developing that walled community in 1956, so many homes nearby date to the late 1950s and 1960s, when galvanized and early copper supply lines were standard.

For a homeowner that means a predictable list of calls. Pinhole leaks in copper, rusty hot water from aging galvanized lines, and original water heaters that have outlived their design life. When you describe the house, tell the dispatcher roughly when it was built, because it tells us what to bring.

What makes work in Los Alamitos different

The city is home to the Joint Forces Training Base, the USA Water Polo National Aquatic Center and UCI Health Los Alamitos, which is its top employer. Those large sites manage their own plumbing. Our calls come from homes, small offices and shops along the city's main streets, where access and parking matter to a fast response.

The community traces its start to a sugar beet factory that the Bixby family founded in 1896, which shows how recent most of this development is. The land turned from farm to suburb within living memory, and much of its plumbing is now at the age where replacement planning pays off.

Water and permits for Los Alamitos homeowners

Golden State Water Company serves Los Alamitos. Golden State Water reports an average hardness of 237 mg/L, or about 13.8 grains per gallon, for its West Orange County system. Readings in its 2025 data ranged from roughly 63 to 369 mg/L.

Hard water shortens the life of water heaters, so a heater in a Los Alamitos garage is often worn out sooner than the manufacturer's age suggests. A water softener slows that wear.

Permit rules for water heaters and plumbing work are set by the city that issues the permit. Check with the Los Alamitos building department for current requirements, and we will help you prepare the paperwork.

Water heaters in Los Alamitos garages and closets

Many homes here keep the water heater in a garage corner or a hall closet. Those spaces are tight, and an old tank that fails can flood a finished room beside it. If your heater is more than about ten years old, a drain pan and a working shutoff are worth checking before it fails.

When we replace a unit, we confirm the strapping, the relief valve discharge and the venting, and we haul the old tank away. See water heater installation for the planning steps.

Signs it is time to call before the emergency

  • Rust colored hot water that does not clear.
  • A tank that rumbles or pops when it heats.
  • Damp spots on the garage floor near the heater.
  • Pinhole stains on a copper line.
  • A water bill that rises with no change in habits.

Checking a plumber's license in Los Alamitos

Any plumbing contractor working in Los Alamitos should be licensed by the California Contractors State License Board, and work should follow the California Plumbing Code.

The CSLB License Check tool at cslb.ca.gov lets you verify a license, and the board answers questions at 1-800-321-CSLB (2752).
