Sewer Camera Inspection in Cypress, CA

A sewer camera shows what is in the line without digging. It is the best way to learn the condition of a lateral before a purchase, after repeated backups or before a remodel.

What a camera inspection tells you

A small camera on a flexible cable travels from the cleanout toward the connection with the public sewer. A transmitter marks the depth and position. The monitor shows cracks, roots, offsets, bellies, grease and pipe material as they appear.

You get the footage, so a second opinion or a repair estimate can use the same evidence.

When to schedule one

Before buying a home

Older homes in Cypress may have original clay or cast iron laterals. A camera shows their condition before closing.

After a backup

A camera finds the cause. See sewer line cleaning and sewer line repair.

Before a remodel

New fixtures add load. A camera confirms the line can handle it.

Routine checks

A line with trees nearby benefits from a check every few years.

Reading the footage together

Findings fall into a few groups. A sound line needs nothing. A line with roots or grease needs cleaning. A line with an offset, a belly or cracks needs a repair plan. We tell you which group yours is in and what each path involves.

How the visit runs

The technician locates the cleanout, sets up the reel and feeds the camera while a monitor shows the pipe. A locator on the surface marks depth and any problems so a repair, if needed, can be planned. The visit ends with a review of the footage and a written summary.

If the line is too full to inspect, we clear it first. That is a separate step and is priced and explained before we start.

Using the inspection when you buy

A camera inspection gives a buyer leverage. If the footage shows roots at the joints or a belly in the line, you can ask the seller to repair the pipe, adjust the price or hold funds back until the work is done. Without it, the cost of a failed lateral lands on you after closing.

We deliver the footage and a plain summary so your agent and lender can read it without technical knowledge.

What the camera cannot do

A camera shows the inside of the pipe. It does not repair it, and it cannot see beyond a full blockage. If the line is stopped, we clean it first and inspect after. We also cannot say how long a line will last, though the pipe material and the damage seen allow a reasoned estimate.

How to prepare for the visit

  • Locate the cleanout and clear the area around it.
  • Tell us about any past backups or repairs.
  • Plan for the water to be used lightly during the visit.
  • Ask for the video and the summary in writing.
  • Share the report with anyone helping you decide on the purchase.

Standards and checking a plumber's license

Plumbing work for sewer inspection in California is governed by the California Plumbing Code, and plumbing contractors must hold a license from the Contractors State License Board.

You can look up any contractor on the CSLB License Check tool at cslb.ca.gov, or call 1-800-321-CSLB (2752). Ask for our license details when you call and check them yourself before work begins.
