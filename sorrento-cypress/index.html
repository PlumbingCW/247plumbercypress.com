What Sorrento is and where it sits

Sorrento is a master planned community in Cypress, ZIP 90630. Local real estate profiles say Fieldstone Homes built it between 1991 and 1996, with about 670 homes in four collections. Those collections offer 17 floor plans from roughly 2,300 to 3,800 square feet, with four or five bedrooms.

Decorative entry walls mark the community at Ball Road and Moody Street, and listings call it the Collection at Sorrento. Its streets carry Italian names such as Corsica Drive and Firenza Drive. We found no published boundary map, so we describe the area by those anchor streets and ask for your cross street when you call.

Why larger homes ask more of a water heater

Homes of 2,300 to 3,800 square feet with several bathrooms usually run more showers, more laundry and more dishwashing than a small house. That is general plumbing arithmetic and not a finding about Sorrento itself. It does mean water heater capacity deserves a look whenever a unit is replaced.

A single tank can run out of hot water when two bathrooms and a washer run together. A larger tank, two units or a tankless water heater can fit that demand. Our water heater replacement page explains the options.

Homes that are about thirty years old

Homes finished between 1991 and 1996 are now roughly thirty years old. We do not assume what is inside any wall, because pipe materials differ from house to house and many have had work done. A visit, a pressure check or a camera run shows what you actually have.

The checklist below is general guidance for any home of this age.

A simple plumbing checkup for a home of this age

  • Find the main shutoff and turn it off and on once to confirm it still moves.
  • Look at the water heater for rust at the base, moisture on the floor or a very old date on its label.
  • Check the shutoff valves under sinks and toilets for slow drips.
  • Watch your water bill, since an unexplained rise can point to a hidden leak.
  • Note any slow drain so it can be cleared before it becomes a backup.

Water hardness and permits

Golden State Water Company supplies Cypress. Its 2025 data put average hardness in the West Orange County system near 237 mg/L, about 13.8 grains per gallon, with sample readings from roughly 63 to 369 mg/L. Scale builds on heating elements and fixtures, and a water softener or filter is one way to slow it.

Cypress city rules put water heaters on the list of appliances that need a building permit. The Building Division at (714) 229-6730 confirms current steps. If your home belongs to an owners association, check its rules before work that touches exterior lines.

Standards and checking a license

Before you hire a plumber for a home of this size, check the license yourself. California plumbing work follows the California Plumbing Code, and contractors are licensed by the Contractors State License Board. Use the license lookup at cslb.ca.gov or call 1-800-321-CSLB (2752), whoever you choose.
