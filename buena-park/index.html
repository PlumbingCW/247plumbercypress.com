A big city with big plumbing variety

Buena Park was incorporated on January 27, 1953 and covers about 10.6 square miles with roughly 84,000 residents. It has single family neighborhoods, apartment communities, strip malls and the entertainment district around Knott's Berry Farm, The Source OC and Medieval Times.

That variety makes for a wide call list. A house on a quiet street needs a water heater. An apartment building needs a main line cleared. A restaurant near Beach Boulevard needs its grease line jetted before the dinner rush.

Getting around Buena Park

Buena Park is crossed by the I-5 and SR-91 freeways, and by major streets such as Beach Boulevard, Orangethorpe Avenue, Artesia Boulevard, Valley View Street and Knott Avenue. The SR-91 splits the city into northern and southern halves, and Cypress sits along its southwest edge.

Because of that geography, we dispatch by street and cross street, not only by ZIP code. Giving the dispatcher your cross street trims minutes off the response.

Commercial and entertainment district plumbing

The area around Knott's Berry Farm, The Source OC and Buena Park Downtown has kitchens, restrooms and water heaters running long hours. A failure in the middle of a busy weekend costs money, so we prioritize keeping those operations working.

Restaurants and shops can also arrange scheduled visits for jetting, trap checks and fixture repair. The hydro jetting page describes how a planned schedule works.

Apartment communities and shared lines

Large apartment communities share main drains. A backup there affects many units at once, so we send a crew with a heavy machine and a camera and clear the line from the nearest cleanout. After the line is open, we explain whether the cause was grease, roots or a damaged section.

What property managers should have on hand

  • The location of each building's main shutoff.
  • A map of cleanouts.
  • An after hours contact who can authorize work.
  • A list of past backups and repairs.
  • Tenant contact instructions for emergencies.

Checking a plumber's license in Buena Park

Any plumbing contractor working in Buena Park should be licensed by the California Contractors State License Board, and work should follow the California Plumbing Code.

The CSLB License Check tool at cslb.ca.gov lets you verify a license, and the board answers questions at 1-800-321-CSLB (2752).

Permits and neighboring cities

Permit requirements are set by the city that issues the permit. Check with the City of Buena Park building division for current rules, and we will help assemble the information needed. We also serve Stanton, La Palma and Los Alamitos from the same base.
