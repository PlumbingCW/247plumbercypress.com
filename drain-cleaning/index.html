When a clog turns into an emergency

A slow bathroom sink is a nuisance. Water rising in the shower when the washing machine drains is a main line warning. Sewage in a floor drain or a toilet that gurgles when you run the sink means the main is blocked, and the house cannot be used safely until it clears.

Describe which fixtures are affected. One fixture points to a local clog. Several fixtures point to the main, and that tells the technician which tools to load.

Drain problems we clear

Kitchen sink

Grease, food scraps and soap residue build a hard coating inside the line. A snake opens it and a jetter cleans the walls.

Bathroom sink and tub

Hair and soap scum catch near the trap. These clear quickly with a small cable.

Laundry line

Lint and detergent residue narrow a small pipe. A standpipe overflow is the warning.

Main sewer cleanout

Roots, grease and collapsed sections stop the whole house. See sewer line cleaning and hydro jetting.

Snake, jet or camera: how we choose

A cable machine pushes through a blockage and is the right tool for most single fixture clogs. It punches a hole but leaves residue on the pipe wall. A hydro jetter uses high pressure water to scour the walls clean, which matters for grease and roots. A camera shows what is actually in the line before anyone guesses.

We start with the least invasive tool that will do the job and move up only if the symptoms say we should.

What to do while you wait

  • Stop running sinks, showers, dishwashers and washing machines.
  • Do not pour chemical drain cleaners into a fully blocked line. They sit in the pipe and can burn a technician.
  • Keep children and pets out of any area where wastewater has surfaced.
  • Locate the sewer cleanout, usually a capped pipe outside near the foundation, and clear access to it.
  • Photograph any wastewater on the floor for your insurer.

What the older pipes in Cypress mean for drains

Many Cypress neighborhoods were built between the late 1950s and the 1970s. Homes from that era may still have cast iron or clay sewer laterals that have been patched or have root intrusion at the joints. Mature trees along older streets add pressure. A drain that clogs twice a year deserves a camera inspection, not a third cabling.

The regional trunk sewer that serves Cypress, Los Alamitos and La Palma is also being rehabilitated by the Orange County Sanitation District, but that line is separate from the lateral that serves your home.

Standards and checking a plumber's license

Plumbing work for drain cleaning in California is governed by the California Plumbing Code, and plumbing contractors must hold a license from the Contractors State License Board.

You can look up any contractor on the CSLB License Check tool at cslb.ca.gov, or call 1-800-321-CSLB (2752). Ask for our license details when you call and check them yourself before work begins.
