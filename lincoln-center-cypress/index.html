About Lincoln Center

Lincoln Center is described by local listings as an all age manufactured home community on Bloomfield Avenue, with a pool and clubhouse. The City of Cypress housing plan lists Lincoln Center Mobile Home Park with 306 spaces, and a second smaller park elsewhere in the city. This page is about Lincoln Center only.

The same housing plan counts about 421 mobile homes and similar units in the whole city, all in two parks. That makes Lincoln Center a distinct, easily named community, and a small slice of the city's housing.

Park lines and your lines

In many manufactured home communities the park owns the main utility lines up to a connection point near each home, and the resident owns what lies beyond it. Where that point sits differs from park to park. That is general information, so ask park management for the rule that applies to your space.

It matters because a leak on the park side is a park repair, and a leak on your side is yours. A plumber can find where the water is coming from, and your park documents decide who pays.

What is different about plumbing in a manufactured home

Pipes in a manufactured home often run beneath the floor or behind skirting, so access can be tight. Materials differ from one home to another, so we ask what you have and do not assume. Photos of the area under the home help a lot.

If you rent the home instead of owning it, your landlord or the home owner authorizes the repair. Stop the water, then call the owner as well as us.

Hard water and permits

Water in Cypress is supplied by Golden State Water, and its latest report lists average hardness near 13.8 grains per gallon. Small water heaters feel that hardness most, so a softener or filter may be worth a look.

Before replacing a water heater, ask park management about park rules and call the City of Cypress Building Division at (714) 229-6730 to confirm whether a permit applies. We do not assume the answer for any single home.

Standards and checking a license

Check any contractor before work begins. The California Plumbing Code sets the technical rules, and the Contractors State License Board offers a license lookup at cslb.ca.gov or at 1-800-321-CSLB (2752). Park management may also ask who is doing the work.
