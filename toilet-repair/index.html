What to do if a toilet is overflowing

Resist the urge to flush again. Remove the tank lid, lift the flapper or push the float down and close the supply valve, which is the small oval handle on the wall or floor behind the toilet. Turn it clockwise until it stops. That stops new water from entering the bowl.

If the toilet is overflowing because the main drain is blocked, the problem is not the toilet. Describe whether other fixtures are slow, because the answer determines whether the technician needs a toilet auger or a main line machine.

Toilet problems we fix

Clogs

An auger clears the trap. If the clog is deeper than the toilet, we check the drain line, see drain cleaning.

Running toilet

A worn flapper, fill valve or chain wastes water silently. Replacing the parts costs less than the water bill.

Leak at the base

A failing wax ring or loose closet bolts let water escape around the base. Left alone, it rots the subfloor.

Weak flush

Mineral deposits in the rim holes and a worn flush valve reduce power.

Repair or replace the toilet

A toilet older than about twenty years, or one with a cracked bowl or tank, is a candidate for replacement. Older high flow models also use far more water per flush than current fixtures. If the toilet is sound and the fault is a part, repair is the better value.

Hard water leaves scale on fill valves and flush parts, which shortens their life. Golden State Water reports an average hardness of 237 mg/L, or about 13.8 grains per gallon, for its West Orange County system. Readings in its 2025 data ranged from roughly 63 to 369 mg/L.

First aid for a toilet emergency

  • Close the shutoff valve behind or beside the toilet.
  • Lift the tank lid and push the flapper down if the tank keeps refilling.
  • Do not use chemical cleaners in a fully blocked toilet.
  • Put towels around the base and open a window for ventilation.
  • Photograph any water that reached the floor for your insurer.

Water use and the case for a newer toilet

Toilets are among the biggest water users in a home. Older models can use several gallons per flush, while current fixtures are designed for far less. Replacing an old toilet with an efficient model can lower the bill, and the savings are larger in a household with many people.

When we replace a toilet, we also check the shutoff valve, the supply line and the floor flange. A fresh toilet on a rusted valve or a cracked flange is a repair that will fail again.

Standards and checking a plumber's license

Plumbing work for toilet repair in California is governed by the California Plumbing Code, and plumbing contractors must hold a license from the Contractors State License Board.

You can look up any contractor on the CSLB License Check tool at cslb.ca.gov, or call 1-800-321-CSLB (2752). Ask for our license details when you call and check them yourself before work begins.
