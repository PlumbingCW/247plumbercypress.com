Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026. This policy explains what information we handle when you call us or visit this website.

Information we collect

This website does not have forms. We receive information when you call us, such as your name, phone number, address and the details of your plumbing problem.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use it

We use call information to dispatch technicians, give you a price, perform the work, follow up and improve our service. We do not sell your personal information.

Third party content

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and maps from Google Maps. Those services receive technical information such as your IP address when the content loads. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

Your choices

You may ask us what information we hold about you or ask us to correct or delete it, subject to our legal and record keeping duties. Contact us at contact@247plumbercypress.com or call (888) 910-8644.

Changes
