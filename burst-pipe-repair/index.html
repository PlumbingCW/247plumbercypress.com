The first minutes after a pipe lets go

Most of the damage from a burst pipe comes from the water that runs after the break, not the break itself. A half inch line under normal household pressure can release hundreds of gallons in an hour. That is why the shutoff valve matters more than anything else you can do in the first five minutes.

The main valve is usually at the front of the house near the meter, at a hose bib on the side wall, or in the garage near the water heater. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If it will not turn, tell the dispatcher, because a stuck valve changes what the technician needs to bring.

Before the plumber arrives

Shut the water

Close the main valve. Then open the lowest faucet and the highest faucet to bleed the system.

Cut the power if wet

If water reaches outlets, light fixtures or the panel, switch off those breakers from a dry spot. Do not stand in water.

Protect what you can

Move furniture and electronics, lift rugs and place towels. Photograph everything before cleanup for your insurer.

Note the location

Tell us where water first appeared and any hissing, banging or pressure drops you noticed earlier.

Why pipes burst in Southern California homes

Freezing is rare in Orange County, so Cypress bursts usually come from age, corrosion, high pressure or ground movement. Galvanized steel lines from the 1950s and 1960s rust from the inside. Copper develops pinhole leaks that grow into splits. Pressure above about 80 psi strains every fitting in the house, and a failed pressure regulator is a common hidden cause.

Cypress grew from dairy farmland into a suburb during the 1960s, and much of the housing from that era still has original supply plumbing somewhere in the walls or under the slab. A burst in one section often tells you the rest of the line is close behind.

What shapes the cost of a burst pipe repair

We explain the price before any cutting begins. The usual drivers are:

Pipe materialCopper, PEX, CPVC and galvanized each need different fittings and labor.
LocationA wall or ceiling needs opening and patching. An exposed garage line does not.
Length of damageA short split is a small job. A corroded run is a bigger one.
Pressure and valvesReplacing a failed regulator or shutoff may be part of the fix.

Standards and checking a plumber's license

Plumbing work for burst pipe repair in California is governed by the California Plumbing Code, and plumbing contractors must hold a license from the Contractors State License Board.

You can look up any contractor on the CSLB License Check tool at cslb.ca.gov, or call 1-800-321-CSLB (2752). Ask for our license details when you call and check them yourself before work begins.

After the repair: drying and the next steps

We repair and test the line, but drying the structure is a separate job. If walls, carpet or subfloor are wet, a water damage contractor should dry them within a day or two to stop mold. Our crew can point out where moisture is likely to hide.
