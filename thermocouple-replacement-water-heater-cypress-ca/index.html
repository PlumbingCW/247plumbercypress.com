Thermocouple Replacement on a Water Heater: A Cypress, CA Homeowner Guide

By the Cypress 24/7 Plumbing team. Published November 12, 2026.

If the pilot on your gas water heater lights but goes out as soon as you let go of the button, the thermocouple is the most likely culprit. It is a small safety sensor that proves the pilot flame is burning, and a worn one tells the gas valve to shut off.

Replacing it is one of the least expensive repairs on a water heater, but it involves a gas connection, so many Cypress homeowners hand it to a plumber. This guide explains how to confirm the cause, what the repair involves and when the problem is something else.

What a thermocouple does

A thermocouple is a short metal probe that sits in the pilot flame. Heat from the flame generates a tiny electric current, and that current holds the gas valve open. If the flame goes out, the current stops and the valve closes, so gas cannot flow into an unlit burner.

Over the years the tip gets coated with soot and the metal wears, and the current weakens. The result is the classic symptom: the pilot lights while you hold the button and dies when you release it.

Signs that point to the thermocouple

A few symptoms narrow it down. The pilot will not stay lit after you release the control knob. The flame looks small, yellow or lazy instead of a steady blue cone. The tip of the thermocouple is blackened. The heater is more than about eight years old and has never had the part replaced.

A dirty pilot tube, a draft that blows the flame out and a failing gas control valve can look similar. That is why a technician tests the part before swapping it.

What replacement involves

The technician turns off the gas, removes the burner access panel, unscrews the thermocouple from the gas control and from the pilot bracket, and installs a matching part. The new probe is positioned in the flame, the gas is restored and the pilot is relit. Then the burner, the flame pattern and the venting are checked.

The job is short. The value of a professional visit is that the same technician looks at the rest of the heater while the panel is open. Rust at the base, scale in the drain valve and a corroded relief valve are all visible from there.

Safety note on gas appliances

If you ever smell gas, do not light anything and do not use switches. Leave the house and call the gas utility and 911 from outside. Never work on a gas connection unless you are trained to test it for leaks. A soapy water test on every joint is the minimum, and a professional uses a combustible gas detector.

Cypress water and your pilot assembly

Pilot assemblies and burner ports collect more scale and sediment when the water is hard. Golden State Water reports an average hardness of 237 mg/L, or about 13.8 grains per gallon, for its West Orange County system. Readings in its 2025 data ranged from roughly 63 to 369 mg/L.

A heater that has never been flushed will have a bed of sediment at the bottom of the tank, and that insulates the water from the burner and makes it run longer and hotter. A yearly drain and flush is cheap protection, and the water heater repair page explains what a service visit covers.

Permits and water heaters in Cypress

Replacing a thermocouple is a repair, not a replacement of the appliance. The City of Cypress Building Division lists water heaters among the fixed appliances that need a building permit, so a full swap is a different matter. Its inspections run Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. with about two days of notice, and the office can be reached at (714) 229-6730.

If your tank is old and the thermocouple is only the first failure, ask whether replacing the heater would be the better investment. The water heater replacement page describes that decision. Neighbors in Los Alamitos and Stanton ask the same questions, and our crews reach both from Cypress.

When to stop and call a plumber

Stop if the part will not thread cleanly, if you see rust on the burner or a cracked tank, if the gas control looks damaged or if the flue is blocked. Stop if you are not certain how to test for gas. A plumber can finish the job safely and confirm the venting, which protects the household from carbon monoxide.

If there is no hot water at all and it is after hours, our 24/7 line can send a technician. The installation page covers what a replacement visit looks like when repair is not enough.
